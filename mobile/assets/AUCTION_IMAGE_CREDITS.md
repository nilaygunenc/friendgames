The four bundled dish crops in `food-iskender.png`, `food-adana.png`, `food-lahmacun.png`, and `food-manti.png` were cropped from the user's supplied reference image. Additional bundled artwork is generated locally by `scripts/generate-local-artwork.cjs` and does not require internet access.
